Myanmar seasonal context

During peak hot season (March–April), average monthly temperatures commonly reach around 30–35°C. In Naypyidaw, mean daily highs can reach about 38°C in April. Use this as seasonal context — your selected city's live forecast may be different.

Climate sources: Myanmar Climate and Climate of Myanmar. SUNNR's temperature bands are practical planning guides, not official warning thresholds.

General heat-preparation reminders will appear when the forecast loads.

Temperature forecast data: Open-Meteo. Forecasts are estimates and may change. Follow official local heat-health guidance during periods of extreme heat.
